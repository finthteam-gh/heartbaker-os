## Audience

### Actual buyers

Buyers are aged 30+ and upper middle class. Most buy as gifts or to bring to parties. They pair the breadsticks with soft drinks, wine or coffee, and some with Western soup. These facts describe breadstick buyers. Café customers are not yet described.
